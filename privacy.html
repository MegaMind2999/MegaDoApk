Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

MegaDo is a personal to-do, notes and reminders app for Android. It has no accounts of its own, no ads, no analytics and no server. This page explains what data the app handles and where it goes.

Data stored on your device

Your tasks, lists, notes, checklists, photos, drawings, voice recordings and settings are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. The developer never receives them.

Google Drive sync (optional)

If you turn on sync, MegaDo asks Google for two permissions:

  • drive.appdata: lets MegaDo create, read and delete files in its own hidden folder in your Google Drive. MegaDo cannot see any other files in your Drive.
  • email: your Google account's email address, shown in Settings so you know which account is syncing.

Your synced data goes directly from your device to your own Google Drive. It is never sent to the developer or any third party. You can turn sync off at any time in Settings. To delete the synced copy, open Google Drive on the web, go to Settings → Manage apps, find MegaDo and choose "Delete hidden app data". You can revoke MegaDo's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

MegaDo's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Location

If you create a location reminder, the place you pick is stored with that reminder on your device (and in your Drive if sync is on). Android's geofencing service watches for you entering or leaving that area. Your location is not recorded, collected or sent to the developer.

When you search for a place or move the map pin, the place name lookup uses your device's built-in Android geocoder, and map images are loaded from OpenStreetMap's tile servers. Those services receive the map area being viewed or searched, as with any map app.

Phone calendar

Calendar access is asked for only if you turn on "Show phone calendar events" or "Add tasks to phone calendar" in Settings. MegaDo then reads your calendar events to show them in its Calendar screen, and, if you chose a calendar, adds, updates and removes events for your tasks in that calendar only. Calendar data stays on your device and in your own calendar accounts; it is never sent to the developer. Turning the option off removes the events MegaDo added.

Camera, microphone, notifications and alarms

The camera is used only when you take a photo for a note, and the microphone only while you record a voice note. Notification, alarm and full-screen permissions are used only to deliver the reminders you set.

App updates

Once a day, and when you tap "Check for updates", MegaDo asks GitHub for the latest release of the app. No personal data is sent with this request. Downloaded updates are installed only after you confirm.

Backups

Backups you export are saved where you choose. They contain your data and are not sent anywhere by MegaDo.

Children

MegaDo is a general productivity app and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact
